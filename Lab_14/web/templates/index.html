Key Concepts Covered
serverStatus
MongoDB's built-in metrics endpoint. All production monitoring tools — Atlas charts, Prometheus exporters — ultimately read this same data.
Threshold Alerting
A raw number is meaningless without context. Thresholds turn metrics into actionable signals — cache >80%, connections >80% of max.
RPO & RTO
Recovery Point Objective (how much data can be lost) and Recovery Time Objective (how long you can be down). Timing your restore gives a real RTO number.
